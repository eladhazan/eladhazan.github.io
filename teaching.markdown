My courses cover machine learning, optimization, control, and AI safety and alignment. The links below collect materials from selected offerings.

### Current and upcoming offerings

Please see the department's [course schedule](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/courses/schedule) and [planned course offerings for 2026–27](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/ugrad/planned-course-offerings-2026-27) for current and upcoming classes.

### Previous courses

- [Theoretical Machine Learning (COS 511)](https://sites.google.com/view/cos-511-spring-2026/home) — Spring 2026. Statistical and online learning, optimization, and learning and control in dynamical systems.
- [AI Safety & Alignment (COS 597Q)](https://sites.google.com/view/cos598aisafety/) — Fall 2023. An advanced seminar on AI risks, alignment methods, interpretability, and game-theoretic approaches.
- [Introduction to Machine Learning (COS 324)](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/courses/archive/spring21/cos324/) — Spring 2021. An introduction to the principles and algorithms of machine learning.
- [Computational Control Theory (COS 597D)](https://sites.google.com/view/cos59x-cct/) — 2020–21 course materials. Control and reinforcement learning, with an emphasis on efficient algorithms, nonstochastic control, and regret minimization.
