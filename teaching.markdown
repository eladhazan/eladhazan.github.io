Selected materials from my courses and tutorials in machine learning, optimization, control, and AI safety and alignment.

### Current and upcoming offerings

See the department's [course schedule](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/courses/schedule) and [planned offerings for 2026–27](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/ugrad/planned-course-offerings-2026-27).

### Previous courses

- [Theoretical Machine Learning (COS 511)](https://sites.google.com/view/cos-511-spring-2026/home) — Spring 2026. Statistical and online learning, optimization, and learning and control in dynamical systems.
- [AI Safety & Alignment (COS 597Q)](https://sites.google.com/view/cos598aisafety/) — Fall 2023. Advanced seminar on AI risks, alignment methods, interpretability, and game-theoretic approaches.
- [Introduction to Machine Learning (COS 324)](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/courses/archive/spring21/cos324/) — Spring 2021. Introduction to machine learning principles and algorithms.
- [Computational Control Theory (COS 597D)](https://sites.google.com/view/cos59x-cct/) — 2020–21 materials. Control and reinforcement learning, emphasizing efficient algorithms, nonstochastic control, and regret minimization.
