Due to the volume of inquiries, I am unable to reply to individual requests. Please use the application routes and program information below.

### Postdoctoral positions in AI alignment and safety

For position details and applications, see the [Princeton AI Alignment and Safety postdoctoral announcement](https://apply.interfolio.com/192161). Learn more about the initiative at [Princeton AI Alignment and Safety (AIAS)](https://alignment.dais.princeton.edu/).

### Theoretical machine learning postdocs

Please see the [Theoretical Machine Learning group's positions page](https://mltheory.cs.princeton.edu/positions/) for opportunities at Princeton University and the Institute for Advanced Study.

### Prospective Ph.D. students

Applications are handled through Princeton's formal graduate admissions process. Please see the [Computer Science graduate program and application information](https://gradschool.princeton.edu/academics/degrees-requirements/fields-study/computer-science) for requirements and deadlines.

### Students already at Princeton

Undergraduates interested in independent work or a senior thesis should consult the department's [getting-started guide](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/ugrad/getting-started) and [advisers and research topics](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/ugrad/advisers-and-research-topics). These pages explain the process and list advising availability.

Graduate students should follow their department's research-adviser selection process. See the [Computer Science graduate program](https://gradschool.princeton.edu/academics/degrees-requirements/fields-study/computer-science) for departmental guidance.

### External students and internships

Please follow the application instructions and eligibility requirements of specific advertised internship or visiting-student programs. These are separate from degree admissions. I am unable to respond to individual internship inquiries.

### Google DeepMind positions

Please apply for research positions and internships through [Google DeepMind Careers](https://deepmind.google/careers/).
