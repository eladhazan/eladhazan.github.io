The volume of inquiries prevents individual replies. Please use the application routes and program information below.

### Postdoctoral positions in AI alignment and safety

See the [postdoctoral announcement](https://apply.interfolio.com/192161) for details and applications, and [Princeton AI Alignment and Safety (AIAS)](https://alignment.dais.princeton.edu/) for more about the initiative.

### Theoretical machine learning postdocs

Find opportunities at Princeton University and the Institute for Advanced Study on the [Theoretical Machine Learning group's positions page](https://mltheory.cs.princeton.edu/positions/).

### Prospective Ph.D. students

Apply through Princeton's formal graduate admissions process; the [Computer Science graduate program](https://gradschool.princeton.edu/academics/degrees-requirements/fields-study/computer-science) provides application information, requirements, and deadlines.

### Students already at Princeton

For independent work or a senior thesis, undergraduates should consult the department's [getting-started guide](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/ugrad/getting-started) and [advisers and research topics](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/ugrad/advisers-and-research-topics) for the process and advising availability.

Graduate students should follow their department's research-adviser selection process; see the [Computer Science graduate program](https://gradschool.princeton.edu/academics/degrees-requirements/fields-study/computer-science) for guidance.

### External students and internships

Follow the application instructions and eligibility requirements of advertised internship or visiting-student programs, which are separate from degree admissions. I cannot respond to individual internship inquiries.

### Google DeepMind positions

Apply for research positions and internships through [Google DeepMind Careers](https://deepmind.google/careers/).
